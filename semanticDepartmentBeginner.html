The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) program with specializations in Data Science, General, Generative AI, and Game Designing is designed to deliver advanced computing knowledge along with domain-specific expertise aligned to emerging industry trends.

The program follows a comprehensive, application-driven approach, enabling students to build strong conceptual understanding and practical proficiency in core areas such as programming, data structures, algorithms, database management systems, cloud computing, and software engineering.
